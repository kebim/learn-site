Building an adder

A half adder adds two bits: sum = A XOR B, carry = A AND B.

A full adder also takes a carry-in: sum = A XOR B XOR Cin, Cout = A·B + Cin·(A XOR B). Chain N full adders to add N-bit numbers (a ripple-carry adder).
